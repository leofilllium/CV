The concept images and `atria-presentation.png` were made with the built-in image generation tool. Their final prompts were:

- **Study Ninja:** A conceptual adaptive learning app on a phone with a learning path, AI tutor chat and progress chart; editorial product photography in sage and ivory; no readable text or logos.
- **Aloqa AI:** A conceptual Uzbek-language voice call workstation with audio waveform and call timeline; charcoal and green studio photography; no readable text or logos.
- **NikohUz:** Two conceptual phones showing Tashkent map discovery and a conversation screen; warm stone tabletop, terracotta and cream, subtle Uzbek textile detail; no legible text or logos.
- **Game Studio:** Developer workstation with a vivid casual puzzle game, sprites and production notes; dark slate desk and cinematic light; no legible text or logos.
- **MediTrack:** Conceptual patient-record dashboard with appointments, prescriptions and a chart on a clinic desk; calm editorial photography; no readable personal data or logos.
- **Atria presentation:** A 16:9 editorial board using Atria's real Today, Train and Diary screenshots as references. Exact copy: “ATRIA”; “Fitness, nutrition & progress in one app”; “Track workouts and meals, review your progress, and get guidance grounded in your own data.”; “TECH STACK”; “Flutter · Riverpod · Drift”; “NestJS · PostgreSQL · Redis”; “FEATURES”; “Offline workouts · Food diary · Health sync”; “AI provider currently mocked”.
