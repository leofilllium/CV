## 5. Interview Strategy & Key Talking Points

When interviewers ask you technical questions, here are your best talking points:

1. **"Tell me about your Flutter architecture"**:
   - Talk about your 3 layers: Presentation (BLoC/Cubit, Freezed states/events, GoRouter), Domain (UseCases, Entities, Failure definitions using `dartz` `Either<Failure, T>`), and Data (Repository implementations, RemoteDataSource with Dio, LocalDataSource with Hive/SecureStorage).
   - Mention Dependency Injection with `get_it` and `@injectable`.
2. **"How do you handle real-time state and streaming?"**:
   - Talk about Server-Sent Events (SSE) in `Study Ninja` for streaming LLM tokens smoothly without re-rendering the whole chat tree.
   - Talk about WebSockets in `taxi_app` and `nikoh-uz-backend` with Socket.io, heartbeat ping/pong, and reconnection strategies.
3. **"Why should we hire you over a developer with 5 years of pure Flutter?"**:
   - "A pure Flutter developer stops at the API contract. When something is slow, broken, or needs a complex schema change, they wait for the backend team. I can inspect the database, optimize the Prisma/PostgreSQL queries, debug the WebSocket handshake, configure Redis rate-limiting, and integrate AI endpoints myself. I save companies months of cross-team coordination."
